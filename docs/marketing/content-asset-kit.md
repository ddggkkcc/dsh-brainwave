## 三、X / Twitter Thread：6 条英文推文

> 主题：随手抽屉 Stash。含"方案/商业模式对比"（轻量就地插件 vs 标签页 / flomo / Notion），数字用真实任务切换研究数据。每条带明确 CTA。

**1/6 — Hook（数据钩子）**
> It takes ~23 minutes to refocus after an interruption.
>
> But every time you use AI chat, you get interrupted by your own curiosity — an unknown term, a side idea, a "wait, what does that mean?"
>
> You either break flow to search, or you lose the thought.
>
> Here's how I'm fixing it 🧵

**2/6 — The pain（场景共鸣）**
> The real cost isn't the question. It's the context switch.
>
> - Open a new tab → lose your place
> - Copy to a notes app → lose the conversation context
> - Ignore it → lose the idea entirely
>
> Research shows task-switching can eat up to 40% of your efficiency.
> Multiply that by 10 ideas/day. 📉

**3/6 — The approach（方案登场）**
> Meet Stash — a "later drawer" for AI chat.
>
> Two moves, zero page-jumps:
> 1. Select any text → it drops into the drawer (no modal, ~0.5s)
> 2. Pull it back → ask in an in-page floating window, with an optional "include context" switch
>
> Capture now. Ask later. Without breaking flow.

**4/6 — Model comparison（方案/模式对比）**
> How does it compare to the usual "capture ideas" stack?
>
> 🗂 Tabs: free, but chaos — and you forget why you opened them
> 📝 Notes apps (flomo/Apple Notes): manual, breaks flow, context lost
> 📒 Notion: too heavy for a 2-second thought
> 🗄 Stash: in-place, keeps context, re-ask in-window
>
> Different bet: capture should be lighter than the thought itself.

**5/6 — Why this shape（设计取舍）**
> Two decisions that matter:
> - It's a floating overlay, not a new app — so you never leave the chat
> - "Include context" is a switch, not a default — because some questions deserve a clean slate, others need the full thread
>
> The hard part isn't features. It's restraint.

**6/6 — CTA**

>
> If you lose ideas mid-AI-conversation, this is for you:
> 👉 [repo link]
>
> What interrupts your flow most when chatting with AI? Reply 👇

## 五、传播原则（贯穿所有内容）

1. **数字标题原则**：标题必含一个具体数字（23 分钟、40%、0.5 秒、3 步、23 个标签页）。模糊词（"超好用""神器""效率翻倍"）一律改写成数字。
   - 反例：这个插件超好用
   - 正例：比复制到 Notion 少 3 步的灵感管理法
2. **每条带 CTA 原则**：无 CTA 不发布。CTA 三档备选，按平台选：
   - 互动型（小红书首选）：你被 AI 打断过最惨的一次？评论区扣 1
   - 引流型（小红书）：想要《防走神清单》扣 1，群聊取
   - 共创型（小黑盒/X）：你的吐槽会直接进需求文档 → [repo]
3. **真实数据优先**：用真实可查的研究数据做钩子，不编造"用户数/下载量"等未发生的指标（项目在规范阶段，无 MVP，绝不虚构使用数据）。产品自身数字只用设计目标（0.5 秒、3 步、1 浮窗、0 弹窗）并标注为"设计目标"。
4. **产品是答案不是主角**：每篇产品植入 ≤1 处，出现在"方案"位置，前面铺垫够痛的痛点。
5. **断更红线**：宁可降频（小红书从 4 篇降到 2 篇）也不断更；算法对断更账号重新冷启动极不友好。
